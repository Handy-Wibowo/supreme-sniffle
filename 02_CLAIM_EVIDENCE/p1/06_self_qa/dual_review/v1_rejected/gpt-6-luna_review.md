C7 BLOCK — Five highest-risk issues, in priority order:

1. **High — Unknown provenance of central A0/A1 features.** Stored features may depend on unknown populations, computation times, or labels, so the near-perfect PhiUSIIL benchmark may not be interpretable as a clean model evaluation. **Where:** [P061], [P081], [P135]. **Smallest fix:** either provide producer/provenance evidence and a leakage audit for every retained feature, or make independently recomputed, provenance-clean features the primary analysis and downgrade A0/A1 to exploratory results.

2. **High — Quantitative evidence and Table 3 are incomplete.** Confidence intervals claimed in [P088] are absent from Table 3, while monthly temporal statistics in [P110] and some additional aggregate claims are not tabulated or plotted. **Where:** [P088], [P110], [P137]. **Smallest fix:** add the missing columns/tables or remove every unsupported numerical claim, and ensure all cited table contents actually exist.

3. **High — The principal temporal test was previously inspected.** The manuscript openly states that the test partition was examined in exploratory work, so the reported temporal and threshold results are not independent holdout evidence. This is not a boundary violation because the limitation is disclosed, but it materially weakens the empirical strength of the generalisation and operational conclusions. **Where:** [P029], [P074], [P106], [P139]. **Smallest fix:** evaluate a newly locked, previously unseen temporal partition, or reframe all such results explicitly as post-inspection retrospective evaluation and reduce the strength of the conclusions.

4. **Medium — Methodological details are insufficient for independent reproduction.** The manuscript does not give complete model hyperparameters, exact split-generation procedures, or enough implementation detail to reconstruct all 85 selected fits from the text, while the supplied artifact package excludes key data, predictions, models, and complete splits. **Where:** [P041], [P063], [P164]. **Smallest fix:** provide a complete configuration manifest, split-generation code, dependency versions, and sufficient aggregate/prediction artifacts or a clearly executable reconstruction protocol.

5. **Medium — Statistical treatment of clustered error categories is knowingly incomplete.** Wilson intervals are row-level intervals, while domain/platform clustering is unverified; the central 37.1% platform-FN finding therefore has no reproducible cluster uncertainty, and the taxonomy uses overlapping categories that can produce misleading conditional rates. **Where:** [P077], [P130], [P133], [P139]. **Smallest fix:** report cluster-aware bootstrap intervals with the exact clustering rule, or label the taxonomy strictly as unquantified descriptive counts and remove inferential language.
